# PACOS — Outro Video Prompts (Kling & Google Omni / Veo)

The outro is **aspirational, not literal** — the *feeling* of a search that's working:
calm control, momentum, a landed interview. ~30 seconds total.

**Google Omni / Veo caps clips at 10 seconds**, so the outro is written as **three
independent 10-second segments** (O1–O3) that cut together cleanly. Kling can often do a
single longer clip — a combined 30s prompt is given at the end for that path.

**Consistency anchors** (repeat in every prompt so the three segments match):
- Palette: warm off-white and slate, one **accent orange (#e84a2f)** highlight.
- Mood: confident, minimal, modern-editorial; soft natural light; shallow depth of field.
- No text, no logos, no readable UI, no faces of recognizable real people.
- Camera: slow, deliberate moves (dolly / push-in), 24fps cinematic, subtle grain.

---

## Segment O1 · 0:00–0:10 — "The quiet operator"
**Google Omni / Veo prompt:**
```
Cinematic 10-second shot, 24fps, shallow depth of field, warm off-white and slate color
palette with a single accent-orange highlight. A calm professional at a clean minimal
desk near a large softly-lit window, seen over the shoulder, working with quiet focus on
a laptop (screen not readable). Slow dolly push-in toward the laptop. Soft morning light,
gentle lens grain, modern editorial mood. No text, no logos, no readable interface.
```
**Beat:** stillness and control — "you, running the pipeline."

## Segment O2 · 0:10–0:20 — "Momentum"
**Google Omni / Veo prompt:**
```
Cinematic 10-second shot, 24fps, same warm off-white and slate palette with accent-orange
highlight, matching the previous shot's light and grain. Abstract visualization of
motion: soft glowing orange nodes connecting along thin lines across a clean pale
background, like a network of people lighting up one after another, flowing left to
right. Elegant, minimal, weightless. Slow lateral camera drift. No text, no logos.
```
**Beat:** connections forming — discovery and outreach compounding.

## Segment O3 · 0:20–0:30 — "The moment it pays off"
**Google Omni / Veo prompt:**
```
Cinematic 10-second shot, 24fps, warm off-white and slate palette with accent-orange
highlight, matching prior shots. A professional stands and confidently opens a laptop for
a video call in a bright minimal room, a subtle relieved smile, warm light rising. Slow
push-in that settles to a calm hold, ending on soft orange bokeh. Hopeful, understated,
premium. No text, no logos, no readable screen.
```
**Beat:** the interview lands — resolve on hope, hand off to the end card.

**End card (add in edit, not AI-generated):** on the final orange bokeh, fade in the
PACOS wordmark + tagline **"Run your job search like a system."** + a call-to-action.

## Kling — single 30-second prompt (if using Kling instead of Omni)
```
A 30-second cinematic sequence, 24fps, shallow depth of field, warm off-white and slate
color palette with a single accent-orange highlight throughout, consistent soft natural
light and subtle film grain, modern editorial premium mood. Beat 1 (0-10s): over-the-
shoulder of a calm professional at a clean minimal desk by a large window, working with
quiet focus on a laptop (screen not readable), slow dolly push-in. Beat 2 (10-20s):
transition to an abstract minimal visualization — soft glowing orange nodes connecting
along thin lines across a pale background, people lighting up one after another, flowing
left to right, weightless. Beat 3 (20-30s): the professional stands and opens a laptop
for a video call in a bright room with a subtle relieved smile, warm light rising, slow
push-in settling on soft orange bokeh. No text, no logos, no readable interface, no
recognizable real faces. Smooth, deliberate camera moves throughout.
```

---

## Editing notes
- Keep O1→O2→O3 on the **same** light direction and grain so the cuts feel like one film.
- Music swells gently across the three segments and resolves on the end card.
- If a segment renders with any text/logo artifacts, regenerate — the end card is the
  *only* place text belongs.
- Total runtime target: intro 80s + outro 30s = **~1:50**. Trim S2 or O2 first if you
  need it under 90s.
